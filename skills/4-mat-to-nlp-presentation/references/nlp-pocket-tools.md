# Reference: Composable NLP Pocket Tools & Exercises for 4MAT Presentations

This reference accompanies `4-mat-to-nlp-presentation` so the skill operates both standalone and inside the multi-skill repository.

## 1. 3+1 Perceptual Positions (`4mat-perceptual-positions`)
- **1st Position (Associated Speaker):** Your lived experience, values, and personal motivation. Grounds **WHY (Connect)**.
- **2nd Position (Target Listener / Stakeholder Empathy):** Step into the shoes of each listener group (e.g., parents, children, academic examiners, executives) to feel their hopes (*KA*) and hesitations (*OD*). Grounds the **5-Statement Yes-Set** and **HOW** usability.
- **3rd Position (Detached Observer / Methodologist):** Step back to view the interaction and evidence objectively. Distinguishes `[SOURCE]`, `[PROPOSAL]`, and `[HYPOTHESIS]` in **WHAT** and **WHAT IF**.
- **4th Position (Systemic / Ecological Field):** Sense the wider family, educational, and intergenerational system ([Global NLP — 4th Perceptual Position](https://globalnlptraining.com/simply/the-4th-perceptual-position-of-nlp/)). Grounds the **Ecology Check** in **WHAT IF**.

## 2. Walt Disney Creative Strategy (`4mat-disney-strategy`)
Modeled by Robert Dilts ([NLP University](https://www.nlpu.com/Articles/article7.htm); [NLP Now](https://nlp-now.co.uk/use-disney-strategy/)):
- **Dreamer (*Sanjar*):** Visual, upward, unconstrained imagination (*"What if anything were possible?"*). Fuels **WHY** and **WHAT IF (KA)**.
- **Realist (*Realizator*):** Kinesthetic/procedural action (*"How specifically does step 1 lead to step 2 via T.O.T.E.?"*). Fuels **WHAT** and **HOW**.
- **Constructive Critic / Analyst (*Analitičar*):** Evaluates risks, missing safeguards, and edge cases (*"What could go wrong and how do we protect the user/child?"*) without attacking the Dreamer. Fuels **WHAT IF (Refine / OD)** and **Q&A**.

## 3. Dilts' Neuro-Logical Levels & The 70/30 Personal-to-Model Weave (`4mat-logical-levels`)
Based on Robert Dilts' hierarchy ([SkillsYouNeed](https://www.skillsyouneed.com/lead/logical-levels.html)):
1. **Environment** (*Where/When*) → 2. **Behavior** (*What we do*) → 3. **Capabilities** (*How we think/model*) → 4. **Beliefs & Values** (*Why it matters, e.g., "Mapa nije teritorija"*) → 5. **Identity** (*Who we are: "ja sam neko ko stvara"*) → 6. **Purpose / Mission** (*Intergenerational legacy*).
- **70/30 Emotional Weave Rule:** In every 4MAT quadrant, devote **~70%** to lived personal narrative, human observation, and emotional resonance, and **~30%** to the explicit NLP model or demonstrator that crystallizes that human insight.

## 4. Sleight of Mouth & Objection Reframing (`4mat-sleight-of-mouth`)
- **Redefine:** Reframe *"Is this just an app/startup?"* into *"It is a human approach to modeling curiosity; software is only one canvas."*
- **Intention:** Validate the protective value behind any critique before answering.
- **Chunk Down (Meta-Model):** Turn *"I can't / It's too big"* into *"Which specific piece should we test first?"*
- **Model of the World / Counterexample:** Show how a first draft that misses the mark is the exact trigger that teaches self-directed revision (**T.O.T.E.**).

## 5. Nested Loops, Yes-Sets & VAKOG Cycling (`4mat-nested-loops`)
- **5-Statement Yes-Set + Lead:** Open **WHY** with 5 undeniable context/experience truisms followed by a curiosity-opening lead.
- **LIFO Nested Story Loops:** Open **Loop A** (deepest personal origin story) in early **WHY** and pause at the turning point; open **Loop B** (professional/mentoring story) in late **WHY**; deliver **WHAT** and **HOW** while loops remain open in working memory; close **Loop B** first in late **HOW / early WHAT IF**, and close **Loop A** in the final **WHAT IF** conclusion.
- **VAKOG Cycling & Milton/Meta Balance:** Alternate Visual, Auditory, and Kinesthetic predicates; use open Milton invitations for dreaming/meaning and crisp Meta-Model questions (*"What specifically?"*, *"How will we know?"*) for structure and testing.

## 6. High Performance State, Spatial Stage Anchoring & Satir Categories (`4mat-state-anchor`)
- **Pre-Talk Future Pace & HPS:** Mentally rehearse the talk associated into your own eyes with VAK evidence of audience connection; activate Resource Anchor + **180° Peripheral Vision** before speaking.
- **4-Zone Stage Map (Audience View):**
  - **Audience-Left (Stage Right):** Past, limiting habits (*"To ne može"*), and *What If Not (OD)* risks.
  - **Center Stage:** Present authority, Mini What, core *What* models, and *How* demonstration.
  - **Audience-Right (Stage Left):** Future vision, *What If Yes (KA)* possibilities, and legacy close.
  - **Isolated Critic Spot (Far Flank):** Used strictly to voice skeptical objections so Center Stage stays uncontaminated.
- **Virginia Satir Postures & Gestures:** **Leveller** (grounded truth), **Placater** (warm empathy, *Give* gesture), **Computer** (calm analytical clarity, *Show* gesture), **Blamer** (directed only at a problem from the *OD* spot, *Chop* gesture), **Distractor** (light pattern interrupt between phases).
