# 4MAT Walt Disney Creative Strategy Pocket Skill (`4mat-disney-strategy`)

Modeled by Robert Dilts from Walt Disney's creative process ([NLP University — Walt Disney Strategy](https://www.nlpu.com/Articles/article7.htm); [NLP Now — Disney Creative Strategy](https://nlp-now.co.uk/use-disney-strategy/)), this pocket skill separates **imagination**, **operational planning**, and **critical stress-testing** into three distinct cognitive rooms.

**Target Failure Mode:** Mixing roles prematurely—either letting the inner Critic shut down an inspiring **WHY / WHAT IF** vision before it is fully formed, or presenting a grand Dreamer vision with no credible **HOW** mechanics or **WHAT IF NOT** safeguards.

## 1. Interactive 3-Room Protocol

Run the user's presentation concept (and any demonstration inside the talk) sequentially through the three rooms, keeping a strict boundary between each state:

### Room 1: The Dreamer (*Sanjar* — What Could Be?)
- **State & Physiology:** Visual upward focus, relaxed posture, open curiosity. *Rule: No "how will we build it?" or "why it might fail" allowed yet.*
- **Questions:**
  1. "What is the boldest, most heart-touching long-term vision behind this presentation?"
  2. "If this idea blossomed over the next 10–18 years (across workshops, physical spaces, and digital tools), what would become possible?"
  3. "What vivid visual metaphor captures the spirit of this dream?"
- **4MAT Placement:** Feeds the inspirational horizon in **WHY** and the *Towards (KA)* vision in **WHAT IF**.

### Room 2: The Realist (*Realizator* — How Do We Make It Tangible Today?)
- **State & Physiology:** Shake off the Dreamer state; step into a grounded, forward-leaning operational posture.
- **Questions:**
  1. "What is the concrete first step we can show or practice *right now* without any complex setup?"
  2. "How does the **T.O.T.E.** loop (*Test → Operate → Test → Exit*) work on a single example (e.g., a parent asking one open question instead of giving the answer, or a child revising a bridge between two towers)?"
  3. "How do we pace the timeline so the audience experiences a real transformation within the allotted minutes?"
- **4MAT Placement:** Feeds the conceptual architecture in **WHAT** and the concrete walkthrough/exercise in **HOW**.

### Room 3: The Constructive Critic / Analyst (*Analitičar* — What Could Go Wrong & What Safeguards Are Needed?)
- **State & Physiology:** Shake off the Realist state; step into an objective, protective evaluator spot. *Rule: The Critic critiques the plan to protect the Dream, never attacking the Dreamer's identity.*
- **Questions:**
  1. "Where could an audience member confuse our long-term Dreamer vision with what our early prototype already proves?"
  2. "What happens if the tool or adult does too much of the thinking and steals the learner's/child's authorship?"
  3. "What ethical, privacy, consent, or developmental boundaries must we state upfront so skeptics trust our integrity?"
- **4MAT Placement:** Feeds **WHAT IF / WHAT IF NOT (Step 7: Refine)**, the ethical boundaries in **WHAT**, and `qa.md`.

### Synthesis Gate
Take the Critic's safeguards back to the Realist and Dreamer to show how the vision becomes *stronger* because of its honest boundaries.

## 2. Output Contract

Produce a **Disney 3-Room Matrix** (in `nlp-pocket-analysis.md` or inline) showing:
1. **Dreamer Horizon** (for WHY & WHAT IF *KA*)
2. **Realist Operational & T.O.T.E. Steps** (for WHAT & HOW)
3. **Constructive Critic Safeguards & Honest Boundaries** (for WHAT IF *OD* & Q&A)
4. **Role-Switching Cues** showing how the speaker (or characters in the demo, such as Lumi and the child) moves fluidly between Dreamer, Realist, and Analyst.
