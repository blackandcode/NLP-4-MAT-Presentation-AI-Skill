# 4MAT Presenter State, Spatial Stage Anchoring & Satir Choreography (`4mat-state-anchor`)

Grounded directly in `4MAT_OCR_kompletno.md` (pp. 12–15, Bilješka 3–4) and `4MAT Model and NLP Research.md`, this pocket skill designs the **neuro-physical process layer (*Proces*)** of a 4MAT presentation—Both the presenter's pre-talk state preparation and their on-stage spatial and postural choreography.

**Target Failure Mode:** Stepping on stage with uncalibrated nerves or internal self-talk, reading stiffly from notes, or contaminating the central authority/vision space on stage with negative problems and objections.

## 1. Pre-Presentation State & Mental Rehearsal Protocol

Guide the presenter through this 3-step preparation ritual (`4MAT_OCR_kompletno.md`, p. 13):
1. **Associated Mental Rehearsal (*Misaono vježbanje / Future Pace*):**
   - Step mentally into the end of the presentation, fully **associated** (looking through your own eyes in the room, not watching yourself from afar).
   - Calibrate your multi-sensory **Evidence of Success (*dokaz o uspjehu*)**:
     - *Visual:* See the warm, attentive faces of the examiners/listeners nodding in recognition.
     - *Auditory:* Hear the engaged questions during Q&A and the genuine applause and conversation afterward.
     - *Kinesthetic:* Feel the grounded calm in your breathing, the warmth of a handshake, and the pride of honoring both your personal journey and rigorous NLP work.
2. **Resource Anchoring (*Sidrenje u resursima*) & High Performance State (HPS):**
   - Recall a vivid memory of deep mentoring warmth and calm certainty (e.g., guiding engineers or building with your sons). Anchor that state kinesthetically (e.g., thumb and forefinger touch or a grounded breath with shoulders relaxed).
3. **180° Peripheral Vision (*Periferni pogled*):**
   - As you stand at Center Stage before speaking your first word, soften your focus to take in the left and right walls simultaneously. This quiets internal dialogue (*unutrašnji dijalog*), opens access to your unconscious preparation so you don't need to read notes verbatim, lets you notice subtle audience reactions across the whole room, and makes every listener feel personally seen.

## 2. 4-Zone Spatial Stage Psychogeography (Audience Perspective)

Assign explicit `[STAGE: ...]` coordinates to every transition in `script.md` and `speaker-notes.md`:
- **Zone 1 — Center Stage (Present / Authority / Heart Connection):**
  - Use for **Mini What**, core **WHY** mission, **WHAT** conceptual models, and **HOW** demonstrations.
- **Zone 2 — Audience-Left / Stage Right (Past / Limiting Habits / *OD* Away-From):**
  - Step here when illustrating adult habits that prematurely shut children down (*"To ne može"*, *"Prvo nauči kako se to radi"*), past obstacles, or the *What If Not* risk of AI replacing child authorship.
- **Zone 3 — Audience-Right / Stage Left (Future / Vision / *KA* Towards):**
  - Step here when painting the long-term *Zemlja čuda* ecosystem (*Kreativni geni* workshops, physical maker space, digital archive handed to the child at age 18) and the final *novi početak* call to action.
- **Zone 4 — Isolated Critic Spot (Far Flank):**
  - Step or gesture here only when quoting a skeptic or limiting belief (*"Ne mogu"*), then step back into Center Stage to deliver the reframe cleanly.

## 3. Virginia Satir Categories & Gesture Cues

Pair each stage zone with congruent Satir posture and hand gestures:
- **Leveller (*Ujednačivač*):** Symmetrical, open chest, grounded breath—used in **Mini What**, personal vows, and final conclusion.
- **Placater (*Pomiritelj* + *Give* gesture, palms open):** Warm, empathetic, inviting—used in **WHY** when sharing family stories (Ogi, Aleksa, Vesna) and listening to parents.
- **Computer (*Kompjuter* + *Show* gesture):** Composed, upright, clear—used in **WHAT** and **HOW** when explaining Disney roles, T.O.T.E., and pilot evaluation limits.
- **Distractor (*Ometač* / Light Pattern Interrupt):** A warm smile, shift in pace, or playful voice when quoting a child's dialogue with Lumi.
