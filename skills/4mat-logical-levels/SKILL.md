# 4MAT Neuro-Logical Levels & 70/30 Story Bridge (`4mat-logical-levels`)

Grounded in Robert Dilts' Neuro-Logical Levels ([SkillsYouNeed — Logical Levels](https://www.skillsyouneed.com/lead/logical-levels.html); [Stellenbosch Business School](https://www.stellenboschbusiness.ac.za/news/neuro-linguistic-programming-series-using-logical-levels-change-coaching-tool)), this pocket skill ensures a presentation resonates from concrete daily scenes all the way up to Identity and Intergenerational Mission—and provides the exact blueprint for merging a **personal journey** with a **formal methodology** (such as a **70% personal journey / 30% NLP Master** blend).

**Target Failure Mode:** Either delivering a dry technical lecture stuck at Environment/Capabilities that leaves hearts cold, OR telling personal stories that feel disconnected from the formal methodology being defended.

## 1. The 6-Level Presentation Alignment Protocol

Map both the **Speaker's Personal Journey** and the **Target Learner/Audience Transformation** across all six levels:

1. **Environment (*Okruženje* — Where & When?):**
   - What physical and relational spaces anchor the story? (e.g., Grandfather's wooden workshop, the family living room with Ogi, Aleksa, and Vesna, engineering team rooms, parent-child *Kreativni geni* workshops, and the digital Wonderland canvas).
2. **Behaviors (*Ponašanja* — What Specifically Do We Do?):**
   - What observable actions make the difference? (e.g., Pausing before saying *"To ne može"*; asking *"Hajde da vidimo šta zamišljaš"*; testing and revising a creation).
3. **Capabilities & Strategies (*Sposobnosti i strategije* — How Do We Think & Model?):**
   - Which NLP tools structure the thinking? (e.g., Modeling without cloning, Disney's 3 roles, T.O.T.E., Meta-Model & Milton questions, 3 Perceptual Positions, VAKOG & metaprogram adaptation).
4. **Beliefs & Values (*Uvjerenja i vrijednosti* — Why Is This True & Important?):**
   - Which core presuppositions drive every choice? (e.g., *"Mapa nije teritorija"*; mistakes are part of creating; true mentorship builds autonomy rather than 200 copies; a child's imagination belongs to the child).
5. **Identity (*Identitet* — Who Am I & Who Do They Become?):**
   - Who is the speaker in this story (son/grandson, engineer-mentor, father, lifelong NLP student at a *"novi početak"*), and what identity does the learner build (*"ja sam neko ko stvara"* rather than an empty label like *"ti si genije"*)?
6. **Purpose / Mission (*Svrha / Misija* — For Whom & What Legacy?):**
   - What larger horizon does this serve? (e.g., Leaving a better planet for future generations; handing a child their preserved creative world at age 18 as a message from their childhood self to their adult self).

## 2. The 70/30 Emotional Weave Formula

When the user specifies a blend ratio (default **70% Personal Journey / 30% NLP Model** for heart-touching defenses and keynotes):
- **Never segregate** the talk into 70% biography first and 30% theory at the end.
- **Braid inside every 4MAT quadrant** using the 3-beat rhythm:
  1. **Beat 1 — Personal Scene (~50% of time):** A vivid, sensory-grounded lived moment from the speaker's life (Grandfather's workshop, mentoring 200 engineers, parenting two very different boys with Vesna, talking with other parents).
  2. **Beat 2 — Human Reflection (~20% of time):** The emotional insight and humble question that arose from that lived moment.
  3. **Beat 3 — NLP Model Crystallization (~30% of time):** Name and show the specific NLP concept or demonstrator (Logical Levels, *Mapa nije teritorija*, Disney Strategy, T.O.T.E., Lumi & Wonder Creator) that turns that personal insight into a shareable method.

## 3. Output Contract

Write a **6-Level Logical Alignment & 70/30 Weave Table** mapping each level and personal story beat to its corresponding NLP model and 4MAT slide/section.
