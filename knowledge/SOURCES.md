# Provenance and reading list

## User-provided primary material

1. `4MAT_OCR_kompletno.md` — transcription of a 4MAT/NLP training handout: printed pp. 4–15 and four pages of manuscript notes (Bilješka 1–4). Direct basis for the five-phase sequence (`Mini Šta → Zašto → Šta → Kako → Šta ako / Šta ako ne`), Kolb's four learning categories, storyboarding (*dijeljenje i raspored sadržaja*), Content vs. Process (*sadržaj i proces*), Mental Rehearsal (*misaono vježbanje / future pace*), State & Peripheral Vision (*vaše stanje, sidrenje, periferni pogled*), Group Rapport & Yes-Sets (*DA-set, pacing i leading*), and the 10-minute impromptu executive presentation blueprint.
2. `4MAT Model and NLP Research.md` — comprehensive research synthesis covering Bernice McCarthy's 8-step cycle (*Connect, Attend, Image, Inform, Practice, Extend, Refine, Perform*), NLP Trainer mechanics (T.O.T.E., Meta-Model, Milton Model, Nested Loops, Spatial Stage Psychogeography, Virginia Satir's 5 Categories, Give/Show/Chop gestures), the 8-Slot Readiness Vector, and the 10-Point Diagnostic Audit.
3. `Wonderland_NLP_Master - Verzija 1.2.md` — Danilo's NLP Master thesis (*Modelovanje kreativnih strategija primjenom NLP tehnika i alata: Razvoj dječije kreativnosti i pretvaranje mašte u kreaciju kroz igru*, Podgorica, oktobar 2026): Wonderland long-term vision, Wonder Creator demonstrator, Lumi the owl guide, Walt Disney Creative Strategy (*Sanjar, Realizator, Analitičar*), T.O.T.E. loop, Meta-Model & Milton Model questions, 3 Perceptual Positions & Ecology Check (the sea-cleaning robot and turtle dialogue), Sleight of Mouth reframing, Dilts' Logical Levels, VAKOG & metaprograms, child authorship, and pilot evaluation design.
4. `nlp-writing-feedback-motivation-about-danilo-journey.md` — Danilo's personal reflection (*Zemlja čuda – od individualne kreativnosti do okruženja koje je podržava*): his personal journey as a boy in his grandfather's workshop (*djedova radionica*), an engineer who mentored ~200 engineers to find their own path rather than become 200 copies, a father of two young boys (Ogi and Aleksa) with his partner Vesna observing *"Mapa nije teritorija"* daily, two years of informal conversations with parents, the vision of *Kreativni geni* workshops, physical maker spaces, and handing a child's preserved creative world to them at age 18 as a message from their childhood self to their future adult self.
