# NLP-inspired techniques for presentations — ethical application & pocket skill map

Terminology and descriptions below synthesize the supplied training handout (`4MAT_OCR_kompletno.md`), the research synthesis (`4MAT Model and NLP Research.md`), the Wonderland NLP Master thesis (`Wonderland_NLP_Master - Verzija 1.2.md`), and Danilo's personal reflection (`nlp-writing-feedback-motivation-about-danilo-journey.md`). Being named in the handout does not establish an empirical clinical mechanism or universal effect; all tools are used transparently as structured communication, empathy, and ideation scaffolds.

| Technique / Concept | Composable Pocket Skill | Relevant 4MAT Phase | Practical, Transparent Implementation | Ethical & Epistemic Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **3+1 Perceptual Positions** (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th) | `4mat-perceptual-positions` | Research, WHY, HOW, WHAT IF | Explore topic from Speaker (1st), Listener/Child/Parent (2nd), Detached Reviewer (3rd), and Whole System/Ecology (4th) ([Global NLP — 4th Position](https://globalnlptraining.com/simply/the-4th-perceptual-position-of-nlp/)) | Do not pretend to mind-read; validate 2nd-position hypotheses with real audience conversations |
| **Walt Disney Creative Strategy** (Dreamer, Realist, Critic) | `4mat-disney-strategy` | Ideation, WHAT, HOW, WHAT IF | Separate uncensored vision (*Sanjar*), operational steps (*Realizator*), and constructive risk-checking (*Analitičar*) ([NLP University](https://www.nlpu.com/Articles/article7.htm); [NLP Now](https://nlp-now.co.uk/use-disney-strategy/)) | Roles are flexible thinking hats, not fixed personality types; never let the Critic attack the person |
| **Dilts' Neuro-Logical Levels & 70/30 Story Weave** | `4mat-logical-levels` | All phases (especially WHY & WHAT IF) | Align Environment, Behavior, Capabilities, Beliefs/Values, Identity, and Mission ([SkillsYouNeed](https://www.skillsyouneed.com/lead/logical-levels.html)); braid 70% personal lived journey with 30% formal NLP model | Praise specific effort and choices rather than inflating identity labels (*"Ti si genije"*) |
| **Reframing & Sleight of Mouth** | `4mat-sleight-of-mouth` | WHAT, HOW, WHAT IF / Q&A | Redefine problems into learning feedback (*"Sada znamo koji dio krila treba promijeniti"*), chunk down *"Ne mogu"*, honor positive intentions | Never use linguistic reframing to dismiss legitimate safety, privacy, or empirical concerns |
| **Nested Loops, Yes-Sets & VAKOG Cycling** | `4mat-nested-loops` | WHY (open) → WHAT/HOW → WHAT IF (LIFO close) | Pace 5–6 verifiable truisms (*DA-set*), open authentic story loops in WHY and close in inverse order (LIFO) in WHAT IF; rotate Visual, Auditory, and Kinesthetic predicates | Do not withhold critical safety/factual disclosures for dramatic suspense; avoid fake yes-sets |
| **HPS State, Spatial Anchoring & Satir Categories** | `4mat-state-anchor` | Pre-talk rehearsal & live stage delivery | Enter High Performance State (HPS) + Peripheral Vision; map Past/Risk (*OD*) to Audience-Left, Present/Model to Center, Future/Vision (*KA*) to Audience-Right; use Satir Leveller/Placater/Computer postures | Present spatial zones and postures as deliberate theatrical clarity, not proven unconscious neurological control |
| **Outcome Framing & Backward Storyboarding** | `4mat-interview` / `4mat-storyboard` | Pre-talk planning, MINI WHAT, WHY | Elicit speaker and audience outcomes (*"Šta još?"*), brainstorm post-its, chunk general-to-specific, sequence backward from final outcome | Ask for consent where decisions are consequential; distinguish speaker goal from audience autonomy |
| **Meta-Model vs. Milton Model Language** | `4mat-nlp` / `4mat-nested-loops` | WHY (Milton open) vs. WHAT/HOW (Meta precise) | Use open, imaginative questions in Dreamer/WHY (*"Šta kada bi...?"*) and clarifying questions in Realist/WHAT/HOW (*"Šta tačno?", "Kako ćemo znati?"*) | Ask respectfully rather than interrogating; frame open prompts as invitations, not hypnosis |
| **T.O.T.E. Iteration Loop** (Test–Operate–Test–Exit) | `4mat-disney-strategy` / `4mat-produce` | HOW | Demonstrate a clear 4-step feedback loop where the learner/child compares current state to goal and decides the next revision | Distinguish an illustrative workflow or early prototype from demonstrated educational efficacy |
| **Towards (*KA*) & Away-From (*OD*) Consequences** | `4mat-storyboard` / `4mat-sleight-of-mouth` | WHAT IF / WHAT IF NOT | Articulate both the positive upside of action (*KA*) and the honest cost/risk of inaction or poor implementation (*OD*) | No fear manipulation or invented certainty; ground risks in real ethical and practical considerations |

## Source-Faithful vs. Evidence-Aware Practice

The printed handout (`4MAT_OCR_kompletno.md`, p. 14) includes traditional practitioner claims about covert commands (*prikrivene komande*), and `4MAT Model and NLP Research.md` documents historical assertions about hemisphere activation and fixed 35%/22%/18%/25% learner shares. This repository preserves the full theoretical richness of those NLP Trainer techniques (`knowledge/NLP_POCKET_EXERCISES.md`) so practitioners can rehearse Yes-Sets, Nested Loops, Stage Psychogeography, Satir postures, and tonal marking transparently—while strictly enforcing epistemic honesty (`[SOURCE]`, `[INTERPRETATION]`, `[PROPOSAL]`, `[HYPOTHESIS]`, `[UNVERIFIED]`) so presentations remain deeply human, ethical, and academically defensible ([Pashler et al., 2008](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/pspi/j.1539-6053.2009.01038.x/); [Sturt et al., 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23211179/)).
