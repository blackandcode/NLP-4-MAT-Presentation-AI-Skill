# Cue cards — "Ask before you answer" (6 min)

**Card 1 · MINI WHAT (30 s)**
- Open: "Six minutes, no slides, three things."
- Beats: a sentence to notice · a question to try · an honest limit.
- Transition: "Here's the sentence."

**Card 2 · WHY (1:30)**
- Open: a house that flies, a robot that cleans the sea…
- Beats: "That can't work" ×3 · best intentions · two boys, two maps ("the map is not the territory").
- Transition: "What should a child's idea hear first?" *(pause)*

**Card 3 · WHAT (1:30)**
- Open: two years of conversations — *not research*.
- Beats: limits matter, but not as the first sentence · the loop: goal → try → look again → decide · praise the choice, not the label.
- Transition: "Let's try it right now."

**Card 4 · HOW (1:30)**
- Open: the bridge between two towers, too short. *(pause 3 s)*
- Beats: notice the reflex · "Does it reach both towers?" · "What do you want to change?"
- Transition: this week, one moment; workshops *could* be the next step.

**Card 5 · WHAT IF / IF NOT (1:00)**
- Open: what if we do → a first try isn't the last.
- Beats: what if we don't → the sentence keeps working · no study yet.
- Close: the grandfather's workshop and a piece of wood → "Ask before you answer."
