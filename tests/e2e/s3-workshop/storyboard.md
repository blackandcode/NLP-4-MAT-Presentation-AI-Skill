# Facilitator guide — "Ask instead of answer" (45 min, teachers + parents)

Status: approved for testing. Format: interactive workshop with nested 4MAT cycles (`cycle` field in `presentation.json`).

**Materials:** printed "child idea" cards (e.g. "a house that flies", "a robot that cleans the sea"); question cards; the one-week transfer card; a timer.

| Time | Cycle | Phase | Activity | Hands-on? | Claim IDs | Debrief / bridge |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | frame | MINI WHAT | Agenda: two short skills, practised in pairs. Voluntary participation; not therapy. | no | C017 | "First, a sentence we all know." |
| 2–7 | frame | WHY | Pair-share (3 min): "Remember a moment when an adult's quick answer stopped an idea — yours or a child's." Volunteers share one line. | yes (3) | C028 | "What could the adult have said instead?" |
| 7–8 | m1 | WHY | Why asking matters: the child keeps authorship | no | C011 | — |
| 8–12 | m1 | WHAT | Three question types: *what exactly?*, *compared to what?*, *how will you know?*, plus the T.O.T.E. loop (goal → try → re-check → continue or finish) | no | C008, C007 | "Now try it." |
| 12–20 | m1 | HOW | Pairs (teacher + parent where possible). A plays a child with an idea card; B may only ask questions, no solutions. Swap after 3.5 min. | yes (8) | C007, C008 | — |
| 20–22 | m1 | WHAT IF | Debrief: "What changed when you couldn't give the answer? What was hard?" | yes (2) | C022 | "Questions help detail an idea. What about big ideas that die early?" |
| 22–23 | m2 | WHY | Ideas often get criticised before they have formed | no | C006 | — |
| 23–26 | m2 | WHAT | Three rooms: Dreamer (no critique), Realist (steps), Critic (what's missing, and protect the idea). An assumption, not a proven method. | no | C006 | "Let's walk the rooms." |
| 26–35 | m2 | HOW | Trios rotate roles on one child-idea card (3 min per room). The Critic critiques the plan, never the person. | yes (9) | C006, C029 | — |
| 35–37 | m2 | WHAT IF | Debrief: "Which room do you usually start in with children?" | yes (2) | C029 | "How will you use this on Monday?" |
| 37–45 | close | WHAT IF | Transfer card (2 min to fill in): one situation this week where you ask before answering. Limits: we don't yet know whether this changes creativity long term (C016); what you notice is a personal observation, not research data. Optional: share observations if you consent (C017). Closing round: one word each. | yes (4) | C016, C017, C022 | — |

**Hands-on time:** 3 + 8 + 2 + 9 + 2 + 4 = **28 of 45 min (62%)**. That exceeds the 40% target.

**Fallbacks:**
- Odd numbers of participants: the facilitator joins a pair.
- Low energy: shorten m2's WHAT to 2 min.
- Someone declines: they may observe and give feedback instead.
