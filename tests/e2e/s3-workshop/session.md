# S3 session log — 45-minute interactive workshop (retarget of S1)

**Prompt:** "Make a 45-minute interactive workshop from this for teachers and parents of 6–10 year olds."

**Mode:** RETARGET at Standard depth. The S1 ledger is reused. Answers are **simulated** (recommended options accepted).

**Facts found, not asked:**
- The thesis defines the question patterns: Meta Model and Milton-style (C008) and T.O.T.E. (C007).
- It defines the Disney roles (C006), the ethics boundary (C017) and the adult reflex (C028).

**Fidelity note:** this workshop is effectively the **first trial** of the C022 format. It must be presented as "a format we are trying", not as a proven programme. Participants' observations are not research data unless they consent separately (C017).

**How the shape differs from S1:**
- It uses nested cycles: a frame, then two modules, then a close.
- Hands-on time is 62%.
- The HOW sections are participant practice, not presenter demos.
- The ethics boundary is moved to the frame: "not therapy; you can sit out any activity".
- WHAT IF becomes transfer plus limits ("we don't yet know whether this changes creativity").
