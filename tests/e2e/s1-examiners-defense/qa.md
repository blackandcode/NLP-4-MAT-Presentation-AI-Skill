# Q&A preparation — S1 examiners

These questions are drawn from the evidence gaps and the "cannot claim yet" list in `evidence.md`, plus the academic lens in `knowledge/FORMATS.md`.

| # | Hard question | Honest answer (claim IDs) | Limit to state |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What exactly is modeled, and what is merely illustrated? | Modeled: a creative sequence (imagine → plan → check) and question types from NLP (C005, C006, C008). Exists: voice-to-drawing revision (C003). Illustrated: the Lumi dialogues (C009). | Lumi's adaptive questioning is not built yet (C013) |
| 2 | Why NLP rather than an established creativity or education framework? | NLP gives operational question patterns and a modeling stance that can be turned into a child's dialogue (C004, C008). It is the starting point, not a claim of superiority. | No comparison has been run; a pilot could compare guided and less-guided drawing (C015) |
| 3 | Where is the evidence that children become more creative? | There isn't any yet, and the thesis says so (C016). The plan is adult usability first, then a small consented pilot with independent raters and a transfer check (C015). | Effects are a hypothesis (C006) |
| 4 | Doesn't AI do the creative work? | That is the central risk. A beautiful AI drawing is not learning (C012), so the design rule is that the child decides each change (C011), and an indicator tracks the child-vs-AI share of suggestions (C015). | Not yet measured |
| 5 | How is the child protected? | Not therapy; the child can refuse, pause or stop; consent and privacy are required for any research (C017). | Consent procedures are not designed in detail yet |
| 6 | Isn't "metaprogram adaptation" just learning styles again? | No fixed labels: the adaptation follows what helps in the moment and can change (C013). Learning-style matching is not supported by evidence. | Adaptation is future work |
| 7 | Isn't this really a startup? | The author's position: "not a startup idea, a long-term direction". The digital tool is one application among workshops and other formats (C025, C004). | — |
