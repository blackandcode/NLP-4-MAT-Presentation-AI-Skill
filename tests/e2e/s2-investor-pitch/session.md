# S2 session log — 5-minute investor pitch (retarget of S1)

**Prompt:** "Now turn Wonderland into a 5-minute investor pitch."

**Mode:** RETARGET at Standard depth. The S1 claim ledger is reused. Only the audience, container, outcome and HOW decisions are reopened. User answers are **simulated** (the recommendation was accepted each time).

## Conflicts surfaced before any question

The orchestrator stops and reports two problems first:

1. **Author intent vs. format.** The reflection states "Zemlja čuda nije startup ideja — ona je moj dugoročni pravac" (C025). A classic equity pitch would contradict the author's own framing.
2. **Evidence vs. format.** An investor pitch needs measured traction, users, a market and a business model. The ledger has **none** of these. Every such slide would be `UNKNOWN`, and the skill forbids inventing them.

## Fidelity issue caught during self-review

The first draft of slide 2 contained two market generalizations that are not in any source:
- "Most AI drawing tools make the picture — not the thinking"
- "Families and educators lack tools that ask rather than answer"

The ledger check caught both. They were replaced with C012 (the author's own caution) and a statement labelled "Hypothesis (untested)". This is real evidence that the generating model drifts toward plausible but unsupported pitch language, even with the rules in context. The fidelity check is therefore necessary, not decorative.

## Blocker kept visible

The funding amount (Q2) is a **user decision that was never answered**. The deck contains `[AMOUNT — to be decided by presenter]` rather than an invented figure. In a live session the orchestrator would ask once more before production.
