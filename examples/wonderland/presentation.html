HOW / APPLICATION

Lumi asks, children decide

Support creative ownership without supplying the child's answer

  • Ask: 'What should the robot do?'
  • Clarify: 'How will we know it works?'
  • Reflect: 'Would this be safe for the turtle?'

Vision is not evidence—yet

  • Propose small, consent-based studies and meaningful measures
  • Ask whether children can transfer creative decisions beyond the tool
