# Academic Q&A / objections — Wonderland sample

**Q: What is the actual object of this NLP Master project: the software or the theory?**

A: The central objective is to model and adapt creative strategies and NLP-informed questioning into activities that help children develop their own ideas. Wonderland and Wonder Creator are one concrete digital demonstration. The educational principles could also be used in workshops or other environments. Source manuscript: §§1.3, 2.1–2.3, 7.

**Q: What does your current prototype prove?**

A: The described prototype illustrates simple speech-led creation and visual revision. It does not demonstrate that children learn more creatively, retain skills, or transfer them beyond the application. These outcomes require a separate study. Verify the exact current technical capabilities at demo time. Source: §§5.1–5.5, 7.

**Q: Why use Disney's creative strategy?**

A: Its Dreamer–Realist–Analyst roles provide an understandable separation of ideation, planning and critical checking. They are proposed as scaffolding, not as evidence that the method universally improves creativity. Children may move between roles. Source: §2.2.

**Q: What role does NLP play?**

A: Selected concepts guide the design of constructive questions: defining an outcome, clarifying specifics via the Meta Model, iterating through T.O.T.E., perspective-taking, and checking broader consequences. These are operational choices rather than guarantees of psychological or neurological change. Source: §§2–4.

**Q: How would you check effectiveness?**

A: First test usability with adults. Later, only with parental consent and safeguards, pilot age-appropriate tasks comparing guided and less-guided interactions. Measure independent child-generated alternatives, revisions and reasoning, child experience, and what can be done without Lumi. Use independent assessors. Source: §5.5.

**Q: How would you protect child autonomy and privacy?**

A: Make the child the author, offer different input modalities, allow refusal and pauses, collect only necessary data, and use separate consent for any research. Lumi is not a therapist or substitute for parents or peers. Source: §§2.3, 4.1, 4.6, 6.1.

**Q: Why 4MAT for this presentation?**

A: It provides an explicit sequence of audience-relevant meaning, explanation, demonstration and future implications. It is a useful planning structure, not proof that people have fixed learning styles or occur in 35/22/18/25% proportions.
