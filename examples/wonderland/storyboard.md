# 4MAT storyboard — Wonderland (15 minutes)

**Selected narrative:** audience-first question → presenter's mission → educational strategy → tangible digital demonstration → responsible future. An alternative for an engineering audience would start with a verified demonstration problem, then connect to the larger mission. In a real workflow the user should choose the narrative.

**WHY re-anchor:** After the role model, remind the audience why the child—not the tool—makes each creative decision. In the conclusion, return to long-term creativity and the importance of rigorous validation.

**Live demo contingency:** If the system cannot run, show a verified video or annotated screenshots, then speak through the child decision loop rather than improvising features.

**Proof-quality check:** No quantified educational effect appears; claims about future 3D or community capabilities are explicitly proposed. Include one or more original screenshots before actual delivery.
