## 2. How the 70% Personal / 30% NLP Master Ratio Is Braided

Rather than splitting the presentation into 14 minutes of biography followed by 6 minutes of NLP theory, every single phase of the 4MAT cycle weaves Danilo's lived human moments (70%) directly into the NLP Master methodology (30%):

| 4MAT Phase | Time (20m) | 70% Personal Journey Thread (Heart & Experience) | 30% NLP Master Thread (Structure & Tools) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **MINI ŠTA** | 1.0 min (5%) | Standing at the threshold between Grandfather's wooden workshop, fatherhood, and engineering | Framing the talk: how a personal life quest became an NLP Master model of creative support |
| **ZAŠTO (WHY)** | 5.0 min (25%) | **Loop A:** Grandfather's workshop. **Loop C:** Ogi, Aleksa, and Vesna at home. Why we cannot nurture children's creativity without transforming the adult environment | **5-Statement Yes-Set**, **Presupposition:** *"Mapa nije teritorija"*, and **Dilts' Neuro-Logical Levels** (Environment → Identity) |
| **ŠTA (WHAT)** | 6.0 min (30%) | **Loop B:** Mentoring ~200 engineers—refusing to make 200 copies (*"dvije stotine kopija sebe"*) so they could surpass their mentor; translating that ethos to children | **NLP Modeling** (extracting strategy, not cloning), **Walt Disney Strategy** (*Sanjar, Realizator, Analitičar*), and **Wonder Creator + Lumi** as one canvas |
