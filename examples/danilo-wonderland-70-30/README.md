# Worked Example: Zemlja čuda (Wonderland) — 70% Personal Journey / 30% NLP Master Emotional 4MAT Presentation

This directory contains the complete, production-ready **4MAT + NLP Presentation Package** synthesizing:
1. **70% Danilo's Personal Journey (`nlp-writing-feedback-motivation-about-danilo-journey.md`):**
   - Childhood memories in **Grandfather's wooden workshop (*djedova radionica*)** where a piece of wood became a new creation.
   - Professional journey as an engineer mentoring **~200 engineers**—realizing that true modeling is never about creating *"dvije stotine kopija sebe"* (200 copies of oneself), but empowering others to find their own path and surpass their mentor.
   - Daily fatherhood with two young sons (**Ogi and Aleksa**) and partnership with **Vesna**, experiencing *"Mapa nije teritorija"* in real life and holding two years of honest conversations with fellow parents.
   - A lifelong direction (*"novi početak"*, not a startup pitch): from **Kreativni geni** parent-child workshops and physical maker spaces to a preserved digital childhood world handed back to the child at age 18 as *"poruka koju je dijete ostavilo svom budućem odraslom sebi"*.
2. **30% Wonderland NLP Master Methodology (`Wonderland_NLP_Master - Verzija 1.2.md`):**
   - **Dilts' Neuro-Logical Levels** connecting family environment to the child's identity (*"ja sam neko ko stvara"*).
   - **Walt Disney Creative Strategy** (*Sanjar / Realizator / Analitičar*) and the **T.O.T.E.** loop (*Provjeri cilj → Pokušaj → Ponovo provjeri → Nastavi ili završi*).
   - **Wonder Creator & Lumi the Owl** as an illustrative demonstrator using **Meta-Model** precision questions, **Milton Model** open imagination, **Sleight of Mouth** reframes, and **3 Perceptual Positions + Ecology Check** (the sea-cleaning robot and turtle scenario).

- `script-70-30-emotivni-govor.md`: Complete verbatim spoken script with synchronized `[STAGE | SATIR | GESTURE | LOOP | VAKOG | TONE]` cues.
- `presentation.json`: Machine-readable 12-slide 4MAT deck validated by `scripts/audit_deck.py --strict`.
- `presentation.html` & `speaker-notes.md`: Interactive browser slide deck and companion notes rendered by `scripts/render_deck.py`.
- `qa.md` & `review.md`: Skeptical Q&A defense matrix and 12-dimension quality review.
